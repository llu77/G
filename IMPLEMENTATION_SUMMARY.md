## 🎨 Design System

### Glass Morphism Principles
1. **Transparency**: Semi-transparent backgrounds
2. **Backdrop Blur**: Frosted glass effect
3. **Subtle Borders**: Light edge definition
4. **Layered Shadows**: Depth and hierarchy
5. **Contrast**: Readable text on glass

### Color Palette
- **Primary**: Sky blue (#0ea5e9)
- **Secondary**: Purple (#d946ef)
- **Glass White**: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)
- **Glass Border**: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)
- **Text**: White with opacity variations

### Typography
- **Display Font**: Lexend (headings)
- **Body Font**: Inter (content)
- **Arabic**: System fonts with RTL support
